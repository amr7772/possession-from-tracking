How this was measured

Five times a second, a detector found the players and the ball in the picture. Players were split into two teams by shirt colour; the team whose nearest player was closest to the ball, measured in player heights, got that moment, and a hidden Markov model smoothed the sequence so a single frame cannot flip possession.

    Limits: these numbers have not been checked against an event log yet, so read them as a first estimate. Distances are measured in the picture, so a ball passing in front of a player can count as near him. Goalkeepers and referees are never given the ball; while a keeper holds it, possession stays with the last team seen on it. Replays and close-ups count as time with nobody on the ball only when the grass leaves the picture.

    Method and code: github.com/amr7772/possession-from-tracking. The same project on tracking data: the replay. The detector is YOLO26s by Ultralytics (AGPL-3.0), run with ONNX Runtime Web.
